Galactic Realms 2.5.2 restores the artwork used before the 2.5.0 bulk redesign for 3,552 units across 71 factions. It retains the 18 individually rebuilt designs, including the Shogun Executioner, the Thor and Colossus families, the Scrin tripods and Hexapod, and the Nod Redeemer. The Allied IFV also uses its earlier chassis when a passenger changes the turret. Unit sizes, gameplay rules, and the 2.5.1 maps carry forward.
